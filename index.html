DotSur is a woman-owned technology consultancy that empowers startups to architect, ship, and scale exceptional products in the AI era. Since 2017, we've been the technical product partner for Colorado entrepreneurs and startups nationwide—from initial MVP through Series A and beyond.

Our founder brings 25+ years of evolution through every major technology wave, from web standards inception through today's AI revolution. We don't just adapt to new technologies; we help startups leverage them strategically to build competitive advantages.

Whether you need fractional technical leadership, AI-enabled product strategy, or a complete development partner, we provide the expertise and network to transform your vision into market-winning products—without the overhead of building an entire technical team.
